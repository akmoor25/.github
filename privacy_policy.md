This Privacy Policy applies to all public repositories owned by [@akmoor25](https://github.com/akmoor25) on GitHub.  
By using or contributing to any of these repositories, you agree to the terms described below.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information through interactions with our repositories:

- **GitHub account information** (username, profile data) provided voluntarily via Issues, Pull Requests, or Discussions
- **Communication content** such as comments, bug reports, and feature requests

We do **not** actively collect personal information beyond what is voluntarily shared through GitHub's platform.

Information shared with us is used solely for:

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Our repositories may reference or integrate with third-party services (e.g., npm packages, APIs, SDKs).  
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties.  
Please review their respective privacy policies before use.

## 4. Data Retention

We do not store personal data independently.  
Any information shared via GitHub (Issues, PRs, Discussions) is retained according to [GitHub's own Privacy Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 5. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect our repositories from unauthorized access or misuse.  
However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.  
If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it via the repository's Issues or contact us directly.

Our repositories are not directed at individuals under the age of 13.
