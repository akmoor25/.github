This app is a portfolio for a job application. This document explains the data the app handles. It has not been reviewed by a legal professional.

This document is bundled with the app, so you can read it without an internet connection.

The operator is an individual developer (GitHub: akmoor25). This document describes only facts confirmed from the app's code and settings.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Stored only on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is not sent to the operator.

  • The passphrase in plain text: the passphrase chosen by the owner who turns a trip into a shared trip. It is kept in the device's encrypted storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain) so that the QR code can be shown again later. It is lost when you change devices.
  • A cache of your trips: trips, steps and notes you have read stay on the device so you can read them offline.
  • Home screen widget data: the trip title and up to 20 upcoming steps (title, remark, start and end times, time zone, transport icon). It is stored so the widget can display it without opening the app.
  • A font cache, and sign-in state and similar data that Firebase creates on the device.

1.2 Sent to servers

When you tap "Get started", the app connects to Firebase, which is provided by Google. You don't need to register an account. Crashlytics reporting and the automatic setup of FCM are turned off in the app's settings until you tap "Get started". The following information is sent to servers.

Passphrase hashThe passphrase in plain text is never sent. Only a value is stored, made by applying SHA-256 10,000 times with the trip ID as a salt.
Notification destinationWhen you allow notifications, this device's FCM token, the OS type (android or ios) and a time are stored, tied to your anonymous ID.
Closure recordWhen an owner deletes a trip or stops shared editing, the kind of action, the trip title and a time are written, so participants get the right notification.

Information you enter, such as your trips, is not sent.
Server logsRecords of running the notification and cleanup functions (event ID, trip ID, counts, error codes, and for unexpected errors the anonymous user ID) remain in Google Cloud logs. Nicknames and trip titles are not written to them.
Connection dataFor security, Google's services handle IP addresses and user agents (Firebase Authentication keeps logged IP addresses for a few weeks, according to Google).

1.3 What we don't collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or advertising ID. The app doesn't include location or contacts permissions, and the advertising ID permission is removed.
  • Usage analytics (such as Firebase Analytics). They are not used.
  • Camera images. The camera is used only to scan QR codes. Images are neither saved nor sent.

1.4 Other network connections

  • Communication with Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics).
  • Fetching fonts: the first time the app shows its text font (BIZ UDPGothic), it downloads it from Google's font delivery service. Google receives connection data such as your IP address.
  • Links in steps and in notes are opened in another app (such as a browser) only when you tap them.
  • This document is bundled with the app, so reading it uses no network. Links in the document (the operator's GitHub, Firebase's explanation) are opened in another app (such as a browser) only when you tap them.

2. How we use the information

  • To save trips, steps and notes, and to show them to participants in shared editing.
  • To tell owners and participants apart and to keep others from reading or writing your trips (anonymous ID).
  • To send push notifications about joining, leaving, deleting a trip, and stopping shared editing (nickname, notification destination).
  • To avoid sending the same notification twice (notification send records).
  • To find and fix the cause of problems (problem reports).
  • To delete data that is no longer used and free up space (the daily cleanup).

We don't use it beyond these purposes, and we don't use it for advertising or marketing.

3. Where it is stored, and outside services

Storing and processing the data is entrusted to Firebase and Google Cloud, which Google provides.

  • Realtime Database (trips, steps, notes and so on) and Cloud Functions (notifications and cleanup): Asia Pacific (Singapore, asia-southeast1).
  • Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging and Firebase Crashlytics: the operator has not chosen a location. The data is stored in locations that Google operates. It is not necessarily in Japan or only in Asia.
  • Notifications on iOS pass through Apple's APNs.

For how Google handles data, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Provision to third parties

The operator does not provide your information to third parties for advertising or sale, except:

  • when disclosure is required by law.

By the design of the app, the following people can see some information.

  • Other members of the same trip: the trip, steps, notes, and members' cursors (the first character of the nickname). Members' anonymous IDs are also visible through the cursor records.
  • The owner: the anonymous IDs of the members of their trips. A notification to the owner shows the nickname of the member who joined or left. A notification to participants shows the nickname of the owner who deleted the trip or stopped shared editing.
  • The operator: can see the stored data in the Firebase console.

A QR code contains the trip ID and the passphrase. Anyone who scans it can join. Show it only to people you trust.

The cache of trips may remain on the device after "Delete account and data", until you uninstall the app.

After the job application process ends

We delete everything promptly after the job application process ends. We stop the notification and cleanup functions and delete all data in Realtime Database and all anonymous users. If needed, we delete the whole Firebase project. After that, the distributed APK can no longer connect to Firebase, and shared editing and saving notes stop working.

According to Google, data in Firebase Authentication and FCM is removed from Google's systems, including backups, within 180 days after a deletion request.

6. What you can do

  • Delete your account and data: with "Delete account and data" in the menu, you can delete your anonymous account and your data on the servers. It works in this order:
    1. All trips you own are deleted (participants lose access to them and receive a deletion notification).
    2. You leave the trips you joined (the owner receives a leave notification).
    3. Your trip index and notification destination (FCM token) are removed, and this device's FCM token is discarded.
    4. Your anonymous user ID and nickname are deleted from Firebase Authentication.
    This can't be undone. Even on the same device, the next time you tap "Get started" you get a new anonymous user ID and can't return to your earlier trips. If a step fails midway, the account is not deleted. Please do the same operation again.
    What remains after deletion: steps and notes you wrote in other people's trips (the app doesn't record who wrote them); notification send records (about 2 days at most); problem reports (Google keeps them for 90 days); server logs (30 days by Google Cloud default); the cache on your device (removed when you uninstall the app).
  • Leave a trip: participants can leave a trip. The steps and notes you wrote stay in the trip.
  • Delete a trip or stop shared editing: an owner can delete their own trip or stop shared editing.
  • Delete data on your device: uninstalling the app deletes the data on your device. Data on the servers is not deleted. To delete that as well, first use "Delete account and data".
  • If it still isn't deleted: if deleting the account keeps failing or you can't delete something from the app, contact us at the address in chapter 7.

What you put in a trip is visible to the other members of that trip. Please don't enter anything important.
